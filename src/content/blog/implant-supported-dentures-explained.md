If you've been living with traditional dentures, you know the daily challenges all too well—the slipping during meals, the adhesive creams, the worry about your dentures shifting when you laugh or speak. But what if you could have the convenience of dentures with the stability of permanent teeth? That's exactly what **implant-supported dentures** offer, and for many of our patients here in Paramus, they've been truly life-changing.

## What Are Implant-Supported Dentures?

Implant-supported dentures combine two proven dental solutions into one superior option. Unlike traditional dentures that rest on your gums and rely on suction or adhesives to stay in place, **implant-supported dentures anchor securely to dental implants** that are surgically placed in your jawbone.

Typically, this involves placing **four to six titanium implants** in either your upper or lower jaw. These implants fuse with your bone over several months through a process called osseointegration, creating a rock-solid foundation. Your custom denture then snaps or clips onto these implants, giving you stability you simply can't achieve with conventional dentures.

There are two main types to consider: **bar-retained dentures**, where a thin metal bar follows the curve of your jaw and connects the implants, and **ball-retained dentures**, which use ball-shaped attachments on each implant that snap into sockets on the denture. Both options provide excellent stability, and we can help determine which style works best for your unique situation.

## The Benefits That Make a Real Difference

The advantages of implant-supported dentures go far beyond just keeping your teeth in place. Here's what our patients appreciate most:

**Eat with confidence again.** Many Bergen County residents tell us that one of their biggest frustrations with traditional dentures is avoiding certain foods they love. With implant-supported dentures, you can bite into an apple, enjoy a steak, or share corn on the cob at a summer barbecue without worry.

**Preserve your jawbone health.** When you lose teeth, your jawbone begins to deteriorate over time because it's no longer stimulated by tooth roots. Dental implants act like artificial roots, **stimulating the bone and preventing the facial collapse** that often accompanies long-term denture wear.

**Say goodbye to adhesives.** No more messy creams or powders. Your dentures click securely into place and stay there until you remove them for cleaning.

**Speak clearly and naturally.** Traditional dentures can slip and cause mumbling or clicking sounds. Implant-supported dentures stay put, so you can speak, laugh, and sing without hesitation.

## Is This Option Right for You?

Good candidates for implant-supported dentures generally have **adequate jawbone density** to support the implants, healthy gums, and are committed to maintaining good oral hygiene. If you've experienced bone loss from years of wearing traditional dentures, bone grafting procedures may still make implants possible.

**Here's a practical tip you can use today:** If you're currently wearing dentures, pay attention to how they fit throughout the day. Do they slip when you eat or talk? Do you find yourself using more adhesive than you used to? These are signs that your jawbone may be changing, and it's worth exploring more stable options before further bone loss occurs.

The process typically takes several months from start to finish, as we need to allow time for your implants to integrate with your bone. However, many patients tell us the wait is absolutely worth it for the freedom and confidence they gain.
